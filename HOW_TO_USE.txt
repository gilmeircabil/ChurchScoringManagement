1. CORE CONCEPT & GOLDEN RULE
================================================================================

  "GROUPS CHANGE EVERY ROUND, BUT SCORES BELONG PERMANENTLY TO PLAYERS."

In weekly church quizzes, players often get assigned to different teams or 
groups each week to balance talent and make friends. 

* The app tracks lifetime cumulative points per individual player.
* A player's score never disappears when a round ends or when they switch groups.
* Example:
  - Week 1: John is in Group 1 and earns 20 points. His total = 20.
  - Week 2: John is in Group 3 and earns 15 points. His total = 35.
* Group totals are simply the sum of the points earned by their current members,
  plus any optional group bonus items (e.g., bringing a Bible, notebook, pen).

2. QUICK-START GUIDE (THE 7-STEP WEEKLY FLOW)
================================================================================

Here is the simple rhythm to follow every quiz session:

STEP 1: OPEN PLAYERS TAB
  Make sure all attendees are registered. If new people arrived today, tap
  "+ Add Player" and type their name.

STEP 2: CREATE A ROUND (ROUND TAB)
  Tap the "Round" tab. If no round is running, tap "Create Round".
  - Pick today's date (defaults to today).
  - Choose how many groups you need (e.g., 2, 3, 4, etc.).
  - Tap "Create". The round starts in "DRAFT" mode.

STEP 3: ASSIGN PLAYERS TO GROUPS
  Under the Draft round, assign your players:
  - Search or tap a player's name.
  - Choose which group they belong to (Group 1, Group 2, etc.).
  - Check the "Unassigned" badge to ensure no player is left out.

STEP 4: START ROUND (ACTIVATE)
  Once grouping is done, tap "Start Round" (or "Activate"). 
  The round is now "ACTIVE" and ready for points.

STEP 5: ENTER SCORES DURING THE QUIZ
  As questions are answered:
  - Tap a player's card to open the Score entry popup.
  - Type points or use quick-add buttons (+5, +10, +15, +20).
  - Tap "Save Score".
  - Optional: If teams brought study materials or completed group challenges,
    tap "Group Items / Bonus" to give bonus points to the entire group.

STEP 6: LOCK THE ROUND
  When the quiz ends, tap "Lock Round".
  This seals the scores so they cannot be accidentally bumped or altered.
  (You can unlock later if a genuine mistake needs fixing.)

STEP 7: VIEW RANKINGS (REPORTS TAB)
  Switch to the "Reports" tab to announce winners!
  - "Overall": Lifetime rankings across all weeks.
  - "By Round": Today's individual winners.
  - "By Group": Today's winning group.

3. STEP-BY-STEP FEATURE WALKTHROUGH

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 PLAYERS TAB (MANAGING ROSTER & DUPLICATE NAMES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Adding a Player:
  1. Go to the "Players" tab.
  2. Tap the "+ Add Player" button.
  3. Enter the player's full name or nickname.
  4. Tap "Save".

- Handling Duplicate Names (#001, #002):
  In large youth groups, having two people named "John" or "Mary" is common.
  The app allows duplicate names and automatically assigns a unique sequential
  tag (e.g., "John — #001" and "John — #002").
  When you enter a name that already exists, the app asks:
    * [ Add Another John ] -> creates a new player with a new badge.
    * [ Change Name ]     -> lets you add a surname or initial instead.
    * [ Cancel ]          -> aborts.

- Editing a Player:
  Tap on any player in the list to view their stats, lifetime score, and round
  history. You can edit their name here without losing any past scores.

- Search:
  Use the top search bar to instantly filter players by name or player number.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 ROUND TAB (CREATING, GROUPING, SCORING & LOCKING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rounds move through three distinct states:
  [ DRAFT ]  --->  [ ACTIVE ]  --->  [ LOCKED ]

- DRAFT State:
  * Purpose: Set up groups before the quiz begins.
  * Manual Assignment: Tap a player, choose a group.
  * Moving Groups: If someone was placed in Group 1 by mistake, tap their name
    and select "Change Group" to move them to Group 2.
  * Unassigned Summary: Tap "Unassigned Players" to see who hasn't been placed.
  * Pre-Start Safety Check: If players are still unassigned when you tap "Start",
    the app alerts you with how many are missing so nobody is forgotten.

- ACTIVE State:
  * Purpose: Live scoring during the quiz.
  * Tapping a player opens the quick-score numeric modal.
  * Quick buttons (+5, +10, +15, +20, +25) speed up entry.
  * You can enter whole numbers, zero, or edit existing scores.
  * Edits replace the round score and recalculate lifetime totals correctly 
    (e.g., correcting 15 to 20 adds +5 to cumulative, not 15 + 20).

- Group Bonus Items:
  * Tap "Group Items" on any group card.
  * Assign extra points for group discipline, attendance, bringing Bibles, 
    song participation, or team challenges.

- LOCKED State:
  * Once finalized, tap "Lock Round".
  * Protects data from unintended taps.
  * If a scoring error is discovered later, tap "Unlock Round" (requires
    confirmation) to make adjustments, then lock it again.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 REPORTS TAB (RANKINGS, TIES & SPREADSHEET EXPORT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Three Ranking Views:
  1. "Overall": Complete lifetime leaderboard (Highest to Lowest score).
  2. "By Round": Leaderboard for any specific round.
  3. "By Group": Total group scores for a selected round.

- Standard Competition Ties:
  If two players tie for 1st place with 50 points, both receive Rank 1.
  The next player receives Rank 3 (1, 1, 3, 4...).

- Excel / Spreadsheet Export:
  Tap the "Export to Excel / CSV" icon to export full score summaries into
  a standard `.xlsx` spreadsheet. You can share this via WhatsApp, email,
  Google Drive, or Bluetooth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 HISTORY TAB (PAST ROUNDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Displays a chronological archive of all previous quiz rounds.
- Each round shows its date, number of groups, status (Locked/Active), and winner.
- Tap any past round to inspect full player scores and group breakdowns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 WHEEL TAB (RANDOM SELECTION / TURN SPINNER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Need to pick who answers first or select a volunteer?
- The Wheel tab allows you to spin a wheel populated with your current round's
  players or groups.
- Smooth spin animations with haptic feedback for fun live audience engagement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 SETTINGS & LOCAL BACKUPS (EXPORT / RESTORE DATA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Access Settings by tapping the Gear (⚙) icon on the top right.

- Export Local Backup (JSON):
  * Generates an encrypted/validated backup file containing all players,
    rounds, groups, and historical scores.
  * Tap "Export Backup" to open the Android share menu. Save to Google Drive,
    send to yourself on messaging apps, or save to your Downloads folder.
  * Recommendation: Export a backup once every month or after major events.

- Restore Backup (JSON):
  * If switching to a new phone, tap "Restore Backup" and select your `.json` file.
  * The app runs integrity checks before applying any changes in an atomic 
    transaction (meaning all data is safely restored or rolled back if corrupt).

- Excel / CSV Import:
  * Allows bulk onboarding of players from existing spreadsheets.

4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & EDGE CASES
================================================================================

Q1: Does the app work without an internet connection?
A1: Yes! The app is 100% offline. All information is stored locally on your 
    device's internal SQLite database. You can run the app in Airplane Mode.

Q2: What happens if a player misses a week?
A2: Simply do not assign them to any group for that round. Their lifetime 
    score remains completely untouched and safe. When they return next week, 
    they will resume with their existing cumulative points.

Q3: Can a player belong to two groups in the same round?
A3: No. The app enforces a single-group rule per round. If you try to assign 
    them to a second group, it prompts you to transfer them instead.

Q4: What if we made a mistake on a round that is already locked?
A4: Open the round, tap "Unlock Round", confirm the prompt, make your score 
    corrections, and then tap "Lock Round" again.

Q5: Will uninstalling the app delete my data?
A5: Yes, Android deletes internal app storage upon uninstall. Always tap 
    "Export Backup" in Settings before uninstalling or changing phones.

5. BEST PRACTICES & PRO TIPS
================================================================================

1. BEFORE QUIZ STARTS:
   Assign players while people are arriving. Use the "Unassigned" list to 
   quickly catch latecomers.

2. KEEP ROUNDS LOCKED:
   Always lock rounds once the session is over. This ensures past scores can 
   never be accidentally modified when reviewing previous weeks.

3. REGULAR BACKUPS:
   At the end of every month, go to Settings -> Export Backup and save the file 
   to your personal Google Drive or email.
